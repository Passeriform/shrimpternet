# Shrimpternet

My personal, warm and cozy internet à la going full shrimp.

> I used to like the internet. I used to live on the internet.  
> I don't like the internet anymore. I want to change that.  
> So I'm going to make it like I want.  

## Why Shrimp

See [r/FullShrimp](https://www.reddit.com/r/FullShrimp/about/) or if you prefer the non-slop version: [Paschimottanasana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paschimottanasana).

Full shrimp is the internet collapsing into a cozy pocket. See [ethos](#ethos) for better context.

## Ethos

I've felt a disconnect from the ethos of the internet, or at least what it posited at one point. It no longer feels like pure joy as companies have shifted to maximizing viewer attention/retention. Doom-scrolling and binge-watching are not part of the internet I grew up with and that's not how I want it to be remembered for the time to come.

My previous attempts at rewinding to that ethos have worked with questionable consistency. I want to create a system that burns away these grubby hands of marketing wanting me to get hooked on things. In essence, slowing down and deciding the pace I want.

This project will contain the DNA of that ethos; to slow down and select a pace at which I want to experience the internet.

## Vision

My vision for this is quite limited. Mostly to narrow down the internet to the sample size of 1 (me).

Contributions are welcome but if it doesn't **feel** like how I want it, I won't accept it, no matter how helpful the changes are.

I would however recommend that if this idea interests you, fork my project and build your own internet as well.

I'd be excited to see how other people experienced the internet :).
